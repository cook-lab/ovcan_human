## CNV method, denominators, and interpretation

The corrected arm method uses the UCSC hg38 `cytoBand` table, downloaded on 5 September 2026 and pinned by SHA256 `ce1b6033a5243e7c5022660b952d2ec33243e307e909afcaeec1894641a5208f`. The inputs and outputs use 0-based half-open intervals. Both centromeric bands are excluded in full, rather than splitting at their midpoint. The five acrocentric p arms (13, 14, 15, 21, and 22) are unassessed.

All 897 model/arm calls (23 × 39) were independently recomputed in Python from the deposited segment table and arm-boundary reference. The assessed segment fraction exceeds 0.783 for every assessed arm; none of the 897 calls fails the >0.5 coverage requirement. The deposited per-arm denominators, overlap fractions, and patient aggregation are explicit.

Patient frequencies count at least one derived model with the event. They are not a frequency of independently sequenced original tumours or evidence that every subline carries a shared trunk event. Both mutation and CNV frequency summaries use this union rule. Per-patient descriptive FGA instead averages available models within each patient before taking the median across patients.

The six highlighted HGSC arm frequencies remain: 20q gain 10/11 (91%), 3q gain 9/11 (82%), 17p loss 9/11 (82%), 8q gain 8/11 (73%), 13q loss 7/11 (64%), and 19q gain 6/11 (55%). Twelve other arm-frequency rows change; for example, 16p gains increase from 1/11 to 5/11 when segments are correctly intersected. The full differences are recorded in `cnv_arm_changes.csv` beside this report. Existing threshold-sensitivity tables were rebuilt with the corrected geometry.

Autosomal FGA retains its denominator of represented autosomal segment length. The HGSC median is 0.635 across 18 models and 0.622 after patient averaging. Other individual model values are TOV3392D (clear cell) 0.671, TOV21G (clear cell) 0.071, TOV112D (historically endometrioid; published dedifferentiated reassignment) 0.226, TOV2414 (mucinous) 0.321, and TOV81D (low-grade serous) 0.021. These results support a generally altered HGSC panel and substantial model-to-model variation. They do not support the proposed blanket statement that every other histotype has less alteration: one of the two clear-cell models is highly altered, and non-HGSC denominators are too small for general histotype effects.

The public reference comprises SRR4039087, SRR4039088, SRR4039089, SRR4039096, and SRR4039097, directly identified in archived normal-coverage filenames. The same capture kit is author-confirmed. Relative ratios and median centring still do not establish absolute copy state, ploidy, LOH, or a composite HRD scar score. A systematic chrX shift was observed, but its origin cannot be uniquely assigned to reference sex configuration rather than tumour copy number; sex chromosomes remain excluded from the autosomal summaries.

Implementation anchors: `scripts/08_wes_cnv.R:88` and `scripts/08_wes_cnv.R:284`. Outputs: `wes_cnv_arm_boundaries.csv`, `wes_cnv_arm_calls.csv`, `wes_cnv_arm_freq_patient.csv`, and `wes_cnv_arm_freq_sensitivity.csv` under `output/`.

## Signature sensitivity and model identity

The original unnormalized full-reference refit remains available as a diagnostic for comparison with previous work. For TOV21G, SBS6 was selected in 200/200 freshly seeded bootstrap fits and the unnormalized MMR-group fraction was 0.733. A restricted reference gave 0.642. These are conditional fits, not proof of an MMR defect or calibrated diagnostic confidence.

The recovered target bins comprise 290,475 intervals in each of 35 byte-identical archived copies (SHA256 `2177970ffdbc933e488067f812e3ae4760310bd9a40fadf4e2fa5389480d955d`). Their standard-chromosome union is 63,465,385 bp. The sensitivity analysis counts hg38 trinucleotide opportunities at targeted central bases, includes sequence flanks at interval boundaries, rescales each genome-referenced signature by target/genome opportunity proportions, and renormalizes each signature to sum one. Observed variants are restricted to the same target union. All 2,417 TOV21G SNVs are retained by the footprint restriction, so its large exposure change arises from opportunity adjustment rather than exclusion of those variants.

The adjusted TOV21G MMR fractions are 0.447 (full reference) and 0.475 (restricted reference), with reconstruction cosines 0.978 and 0.972. This is an exploratory point-refit sensitivity, without a separate adjusted bootstrap. The derived footprint is not a sample-specific callable mask, and residual germline is unresolved. The direction and magnitude of germline-related signature bias cannot be estimated from the retained candidate count. Orthogonal MSI/MMR evidence is still needed for a categorical status claim.

Script 18 was rerun after the RNA agent's exact-index transcript annotation repair. All five DepMap-overlapping models remain rank 1 among 67 ovarian models and reciprocal best matches among the 31 project RNA models; all-gene sensitivity ranks are also 1. BIN67 versus COV434 remains the smallest non-self margin (0.03573). These results support the identity of the overlapping models without authenticating the whole panel. The 30 Cellosaurus STR profiles are external reference records, while current-stock STR and mycoplasma documentation remains pending author confirmation.

The operational SWI/SNF flags still select OV2085, TOV21G, VOA4841, TOV112D, BIN67, and COV434. They combine different subunits and assay evidence; newly flagged loss candidates need gene-specific interpretation and orthogonal confirmation. BIN67 is an established SCCOHT model, not one of the Karnezis reclassifications. TOV112D and COV434 are consistent with published reassignment; the current profiles do not independently reproduce pathology adjudication. The mucinous-origin score remains an ad hoc expression summary, not a trained ovarian-versus-gastrointestinal classifier. TOV2414's published derivation and reference STR record are distinguished from verification of the present stock, and its SMAD4 alteration is a tumour-only candidate rather than confirmed somatic loss.

## Validation completed and residual limits

Scripts 07, 08, 09, 10, 11, 15, 16, 18, 20, and 22 were executed successfully for the changes they produce; parse checks cover these scripts and the three new R helpers. Mutation recovery and arm calls have independent data-level checks in `validate_genomics.py`. Bootstrap input hashing and complete-grid assertions passed; subsequent script-22 runs reused the verified cache. Logged signature warnings are installed-package deprecations (`S4Vectors:::anyMissing`), not variant/reference mismatches. No raw data, v5 manuscript, or external services were modified.

The submission work that remains requires experimental documentation or orthogonal evidence: capture-kit name/design version and vendor target manifest, upstream sequence/library details, current-stock STR/mycoplasma records, and absolute/allele-specific information if LOH or HRD is desired. The exact primary VCF software versions, normal accessions, derived target bins, and missing MAF conversion are now resolved locally and should not remain author retrieval requests.

A lower-priority population-reference sensitivity identified five retained non-driver candidates with gnomAD-genome AF >0.001 despite passing the pipeline's explicitly specified gnomAD-exome/1000-Genomes/ESP filter. This does not change any canonical driver claim and illustrates why “candidate” and the exact AF sources matter. They are listed in `population_af_additional_check.json`; the established primary variant filter was retained to preserve its defined scope, while all five remain available for reuser scrutiny. No project-specific somatic false-discovery rate is claimed.

## Primary external method references consulted

- [UCSC hg38 database/cytoband reference](https://hgdownload.soe.ucsc.edu/goldenPath/hg38/database/): deposited `hg38_cytoBand.tsv` supplies chromosome-arm boundaries.
- [CNVkit pipeline documentation](https://cnvkit.readthedocs.io/en/stable/pipeline.html): capture intervals, references, and target/antitarget processing.
- [vcf2maf v1.6.22 source](https://github.com/mskcc/vcf2maf/tree/v1.6.22): annotation-preserving VCF-to-MAF conversion and transcript selection.
- [COSMIC signature representation](https://cancer.sanger.ac.uk/signatures/sbs/sbs5/?genome=GRCh38): SBS signatures incorporate reference-genome trinucleotide frequencies.
- [SigsPack primary methods paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6720940/): exposure estimates can change with trinucleotide-frequency normalization; either observed catalogues or reference signatures must be matched to the relevant territory.
