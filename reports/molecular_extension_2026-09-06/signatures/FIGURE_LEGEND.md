# SBS3 sensitivity diagnostic

**A,** SBS3 fraction fitted to baseline target-restricted candidates using either the full 60-signature COSMIC v3.2 dictionary (rust) or the 22-signature restricted dictionary (slate). Both dictionaries are adjusted to the same exome-target trinucleotide opportunities. Points show strict backward-selection fits; lines span the 2.5th to 97.5th percentiles from 200 resampled fits. **B,** Identical analysis after lowering the maximum available population AF to 0.00001 and requiring reference-plus-alternate AD ≥20, alternate AD ≥5 and caller AF ≥0.05. **C,** Number of SNVs entering each baseline/stricter pair. All 23 models from 16 patient groups are shown; related sublines are not independent patients. Rows follow the baseline full-dictionary point estimate, with ties ordered by model name.

The baseline uses MAF PASS SNVs with maximum available population AF ≤0.001 on the derived 63,465,385-bp standard-chromosome target union. Missing population annotations remain eligible; neither candidate screen confirms somatic origin. The target footprint is not a model-specific callable mask. Percentile ranges describe conditional resampling variability, not diagnostic confidence in HRD; reference choice, residual germline variants and detection biases remain outside these ranges. A zero estimate does not establish HR proficiency. Dictionary size, fitting parameters, genome-reference comparisons, variant counts and reconstruction diagnostics are provided in `METHODS.md` and the companion CSVs.

Source: `scripts/44b_fig_sbs3_sensitivity.R`, reading `sbs3_sensitivity_summary.csv` and `substrate_burden.csv`. The figure is a standalone report diagnostic, not a numbered manuscript figure.
