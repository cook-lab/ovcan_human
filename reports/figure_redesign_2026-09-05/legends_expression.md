**Figure 2 | RNA library quality, protein coverage and measurement precision.** **a,b**, Each point represents one of 31 RNA-profiled models; shapes and fills indicate the contributing centre, and large hollow symbols show centre medians. The number of genes detected at an estimated count of at least one is shown against pseudoalignment percentage (**a**) and processed fragments (**b**). Detection counts refer to the 22,678 genes retained by the global filter of at least ten estimated counts in at least two models. The shared vertical scale is truncated. **c**, Cross-model interquartile ranges (IQRs) for 8,035 genes measured in at least ten paired models. RNA uses log2(TPM + 1), and protein uses the supplied log2 abundance values. Violins show the distributions; boxes span the IQR, central lines mark medians, and whiskers extend to the most extreme observations within 1.5 IQR. Numbers give medians. The view ends just above the pooled 99th percentile. Differences between these assay scales are descriptive and do not estimate a causal TMT compression factor. **d**, Counts of the 8,427 protein features by the number of plexes with quantified model channels, plotted on a logarithmic horizontal axis. The rust point denotes 70 features without measurements in any model channel; 6,855 features are quantified in all five plexes. **e**, Reported CV medians and IQRs (slate line and band) and bridge-derived per-measurement CV estimates (rust) within abundance deciles, from lowest to highest. Bridge estimates use SD of paired differences divided by the square root of two, followed by the lognormal CV conversion, under an equal, independent-error approximation. **f**, Primary-minus-bridge log2 abundance differences versus the channel mean for four adjacent-plex links. Solid rust lines mark mean differences; dashed lines mark the mean plus or minus 1.96 SD of paired differences. Each facet reports the number of paired protein measurements. The four links use different biological samples; the asterisk identifies VOA3993, an external proteomics-only bridge sample. The vertical display limit of plus or minus 1.6 log2 units excludes 0.191% of plotted observations; all measurements enter the agreement statistics.

**Figure 3 | Expression structure, cross-assay concordance and histotype-associated markers.** **a**, Principal component analysis of the 2,000 most variable variance-stabilized RNA genes across 31 models. Features were centred without scaling; axes report explained variance. Point fills indicate annotated histotype and shapes indicate contributing centre. The two models annotated as endometrioid are labelled. Their placement provides expression context and does not establish histological reclassification. **b**, Raw PC1 commonality components for all 31 models and for one representative from each of 28 patients. Variable-gene selection and PCA were repeated within the representative set. Unique histotype and centre components are the increments in R² after adding each factor to the other; shared R² equals the sum of the separate-model R² values minus the joint-model R². Unexplained variance is one minus joint R². Labels show percentages. These summarize different PC1 fits and do not separate causal biological and centre effects; the source and histotype design remains partly aligned. **c**, Distribution of gene-wise Spearman correlations between RNA and protein abundance across the 30 models with both assays. The 8,033 eligible genes have at least ten paired measurements and nonzero variance in both assays. The histogram and smoothed density are descriptive; the rust line marks the median, 0.400. Related sublines, histotype and assay structure remain in these model-level correlations. **d**, Expression of 25 histotype markers and the MKI67 proliferation control, using one RNA-profiled model per patient. Within each marker, means of log2(TPM + 1) were calculated for each histotype and standardized across the six histotype means. Column labels give patient counts. Solid boxes identify the intended high-expression histotype; dashed boxes and downward arrows identify markers expected to be lower in the intended histotype. MKI67 has no intended histotype. **e**, Absolute log2(TPM + 1) values for the same markers and row order across all 31 models. Coloured points represent models of the marker's intended histotype; pale points represent other models. Slight vertical jitter reduces overlap and does not alter the expression coordinate. Horizontal separators identify marker groups. The dashed line at one indicates the expression threshold used for high-expression marker recovery; recovery is assessed from histotype means, not from individual points. Model-level displays preserve within-patient and between-model variation; patient representatives are used for the primary marker comparisons, and small histotype groups limit interpretation.
