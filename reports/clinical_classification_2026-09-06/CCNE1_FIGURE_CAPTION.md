# CCNE1 multiomic evidence across 42 ovarian cancer models

Rows show exact models, grouped by historical histotype and ordered by descending relative CCNE1 segment ratio where WES is available, then by RNA abundance for models without WES. HGS, high-grade serous; CC, clear cell; EC, endometrioid; LGS, low-grade serous; MC, mucinous; MMMT, carcinosarcoma; SCCOHT, small cell carcinoma of the ovary, hypercalcaemic type. Dashes denote unmeasured assays and are positioned for display, not numerical zeros.

**A**, Relative DNA copy number in 23 models. Rust circles show the gene-overlap-weighted mean of corrected target-only segments, centred using each model's established autosomal probe-weighted median. Open slate squares show the median of seven positive-depth CCNE1 target-bin ratios on the same centred scale. Thin connectors expose differences between the segment and gene-bin summaries; they are not confidence intervals. Coordinates are GRCh38 chr19:29,811,991–29,824,312 (1-based inclusive). Ratios do not determine absolute copy number, ploidy, LOH or a validated clinical amplification category. Exome segment spans interpolate between measured targets.

**B**, CCNE1 RNA abundance in 31 models, using ENSG00000105173 from the corrected Ensembl release-93 import. Positions use log2(TPM+1), with tick labels in TPM. Values/ranks are within this model panel and do not define a clinical overexpression threshold. **C**, supplied log2 normalised Cyclin E1 abundance (P24864) in 31 models. CCNE1 is flagged as an isoDoping target: this denotes detection enhancement, not automatic measurement invalidity. All biological-channel imports were independently confirmed, but exact experiment-specific correction/rollup details remain to be supplied. The source's five unique peptides are dataset-wide counts; three occur in every model and two additional peptides occur only in plex 2.

Assays were not necessarily obtained from the same passage or aliquot. OV3291 and TOV3291G are distinct same-patient isolates and are not merged. The figure deliberately preserves DNA/RNA/protein disagreement and assay absence. See the independent expression review and model-level table for passage, patient, peptide and denominator details. This is an exploratory figure, separate from the numbered manuscript figures.
