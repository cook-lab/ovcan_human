# Additional molecular classifications for ovarian model selection

Evidence reviewed 6 September 2026. This is a research annotation proposal for the Data Descriptor, not a treatment recommendation or a clinically validated classification of these cultures. Canonical analysis files and manuscript were not changed by this review.

The strongest immediate additions are exact-allele curation, a small receptor-abundance panel, and explicit separation of genetic findings from functional phenotypes. TOV81D carries a clinically pathogenic BRCA2 allele, OV90 has a literature-supported non-V600 BRAF deletion, and TOV21G is the clearest model for direct MSI/MMR follow-up. ERBB2 and FOLR1 provide useful, but different, DNA and expression leads. None of these observations requires assigning every model a positive or negative clinical biomarker label.

## BRCA/HRR: the current research tiers must not become clinical tiers

Three independent questions must be recorded: **is the allele pathogenic, is its origin somatic or germline, and is relevant gene function lost in this stock?** The existing Tier 1–3 system prioritizes tumour-only candidates. In particular, uncertainty about germline origin led the pipeline to put BRCA1/2 candidates in Tier 3; this is not evidence that each allele is a variant of uncertain significance.

The genomics audit linked TOV81D’s GRCh38 VCF allele chr13:32371000 AAG>A to **NM_000059.4:c.8537_8538del, p.Glu2846GlyfsTer22**, ClinVar Variation 9328, with an ENIGMA expert-panel pathogenic assertion. The original reconstructed `p.R2845fs` label should be preserved as source provenance and accompanied by the curated HGVS label. Source VCF AD is 75 reference/45 alternate reads, with Mutect AF 0.392; neither this mixed allele fraction nor tumour-only sequencing determines germline origin or biallelic inactivation. The exact record and assertion date belong in the annotation, rather than a flattened VEP `CLIN_SIG` string. [ClinVar Variation 9328](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/clinvar/variation/9328/).

This finding also has directly relevant historical evidence. Abd-Rabbo and colleagues measured the BRCA2 allele historically called **8765delAG** in TOV-81D and reported expression of the retained wild-type allele. Thus, “pathogenic BRCA2-variant carrier” is supported; “BRCA2-null” is not. The historical result concerns the studied stock and does not establish present HR function, but it provides a concrete reason to test rather than assume loss. [Abd-Rabbo et al., 2012](https://doi.org/10.1158/1940-6207.CAPR-11-0547).

BRCA2 A22E in TOV3133D is a distinct, low-allele-fraction candidate and must be evaluated by its own normalized allele and supporting reads. More generally, BRCA-specific expert criteria are preferable to treating any rare coding variant as pathogenic. [ClinGen ENIGMA panel](https://clinicalgenome.org/affiliation/50087/); [BRCA1/2-specific variant-classification framework](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11393667/).

The CDK12 frameshift at chr17:39462437 AC>A has alternate-allele predominance in all four patient-3133 isolates according to the VCF audit. These are four related models from one patient. The absence of reference reads is not an absolute copy-state or phasing measurement. CDK12-inactivated ovarian cancers can have a distinct tandem-duplication phenotype, so these models should not automatically receive a BRCA-associated HRD label; WES relative gains do not establish the structural phenotype. [Popova et al., 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26787835/).

## MAPK: exact variants offer a useful extension now

The retained hotspot candidates are KRAS G13C in TOV21G (clear cell), G12A in TOV2414 (mucinous), and G12S in TOV3392D (clear cell). TOV112D also has a noncanonical KRAS A59T candidate deserving separate allele-specific review. These observations are more informative than a single generic “MAPK altered” label.

**OV90 is a particularly useful additional model lead.** Its BRAF N486_P490 deletion was independently reported by direct sequencing in 2007. A later primary study detected the corresponding endogenous deletion-specific peptide in OV-90 and investigated its signaling and inhibitor response. This is stronger supporting evidence than absence from a sparse external hotspot matrix would suggest. Annotate the exact non-V600 deletion, retaining the distinction between published stocks and current-stock verification. [Estep et al., 2007](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0001279); [Lauinger et al., 2023](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.ade7486).

Clinical biomarker definitions illustrate why this precision matters: the 2025 ovarian indication for avutometinib/defactinib is specifically KRAS-mutated **low-grade serous** ovarian cancer. The KRAS-positive models above have other histotypes. No retained KRAS call in the sole LGS model TOV81D is not equivalent to a callable, validated wild-type result. Likewise, a non-V600 BRAF deletion must not inherit a V600E-specific label or assumed inhibitor response. [FDA KRAS/LGS approval](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-grants-accelerated-approval-combination-avutometinib-and-defactinib-kras-mutated-recurrent-low).

## Receptor abundance: preserve the distinction between target and assay

All ranks below are descriptive within the measured collection, with 31 measured models per modality for the listed targets. They are not clinical cutoffs or independent-patient percentiles. RNA and protein ranks need not agree.

| Lead | Current evidence | Interpretation for model selection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **OV2295, FOLR1** | 277.29 TPM; RNA rank 3/31 and protein rank 3/31. | Concordant relative-abundance lead for membrane FRα assessment. |
| **OV4485, FOLR1** | 192.65 TPM; RNA rank 5/31 and protein rank 4/31. | Another concordant lead from a different patient. |
| **TOV3133G / OV3133-R, FOLR1** | RNA ranks 1/31 and 2/31, but protein ranks 16/31 and 8/31. Both derive from patient 3133. | Useful RNA/protein discordance and within-patient comparison; not two independent demonstrations of high FRα protein. |
| **VOA14993 / VOA8771, FOLR1** | Protein ranks 1/31 and 2/31; VOA14993 has no RNA measurement. | Protein-led follow-up, with missing RNA explicit. |
| **TOV2835EP, ERBB2 DNA** | Autosomal-centered segment log2 ratio 0.985 across 0.681 Mb; 12 positive-depth gene-overlapping target bins, median log2 ratio 0.845; locus-minus-arm value 1.422. No RNA/protein. | Strong relative locus-gain lead. The 0.985 value should not be dismissed because it narrowly misses an arbitrary log2 ≥1 screen; it also is not an absolute amplification call. |
| **TOV3392D, ERBB2 expression** | RNA 216.38 TPM and protein both rank 1/31. Segment log2 ratio 0.578 spans 56.26 Mb, but gene-bin median is only 0.057. | Prioritize receptor assessment. The broad segment smooths across locally different bins and does not support focal ERBB2 amplification. |
| **OV2295 / OV4485, ESR1** | RNA 18.70 and 12.07 TPM, ranks 1/31 and 2/31; highest protein ranks occur in other models. | Candidate ER assessment with substantial assay discordance; no endocrine-response classification. |

The FRα companion diagnostic uses membrane IHC: at least 75% of viable tumour cells with moderate or strong staining. Bulk FOLR1 RNA and TMT abundance measure neither the required cellular fraction nor membrane localization. Cell-pellet IHC or flow cytometry can support model characterization, but should not be described as the clinically validated tissue assay without a validated adaptation. [FDA FRα diagnostic record](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpma/pma.cfm?id=P220006).

For HER2, the tumour-agnostic FDA approval cited here specifies **IHC 3+**. ERBB2 copy gain, RNA abundance and whole-cell protein abundance are separate measurements; breast-specific “HER2-low” categories should not be transferred to these cultures. Follow-up should distinguish membrane IHC from copy-number confirmation by a locus-specific method such as ISH. [FDA HER2-positive solid-tumour approval](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-grants-accelerated-approval-fam-trastuzumab-deruxtecan-nxki-unresectable-or-metastatic-her2).

Published endocrine studies selected ER/PR-positive ovarian tumours, rather than applying a TPM threshold. A research culture can be assessed with nuclear receptor staining and hormone-depletion/add-back experiments; ESR1 rank alone does not demonstrate dependence. PGR is absent from this protein matrix and must be marked unmeasured. [PARAGON prospective study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31227223/).

ERBB2 and ESR1 have isoDoping flags. This method improves targeted peptide identification; the flag alone does not establish a failed or distorted measurement. Keep peptide support and the flag visible. The current experiment used separate SM+iD and pooled-standard channels, so the correction procedure in the published implementation cannot simply be assumed. The upstream correction remains an author/proteomics-methods item; it is not a reason to discard all flagged features. See [the local source-workbook review](CCNE1_EXPRESSION_REVIEW.md) and [the primary isoDoping application](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-28524-0).

## TOV21G: direct MSI/MMR testing has the highest added phenotyping value

TOV21G has 1,416 retained coding candidates, including 392 indels, versus a collection median of 206 coding candidates. MLH1 is the lowest RNA and protein measurement among 31 models; PMS2 protein is second lowest. This supports a focused MMR hypothesis, but relative protein abundance is not a demonstration of complete nuclear-protein loss. Current coding candidates do not establish the causal MMR lesion. The near-start EPCAM frameshift must not be equated with the 3′ EPCAM deletion mechanism that can silence MSH2. [Primary EPCAM mechanism study](https://www.nature.com/articles/ng.283).

The mutational-signature fits reinforce the need for direct testing rather than supply a diagnosis: the fitted MMR-associated fraction changes from 0.733 using the unadjusted full reference to 0.447 after target-opportunity adjustment (0.475 with the restricted adjusted reference). These are method-sensitive mixture estimates, not measured fractions of MMR-deficient cells.

A useful next computation is read-level MSI assessment from the WES BAMs, recording genome/reference assets, callable microsatellite count, coverage, score, controls and threshold sensitivity. A matched normal is not invariably required: GDC uses tumour-only MSIsensor2, while explicitly separating its bioinformatics-derived status from clinical MSI results. Report any new call as research-predicted and obtain orthogonal MSI and/or MMR-protein evidence before describing this stock as validated MSI-high/MMR-deficient. [GDC MSI workflow and limitations](https://docs.gdc.cancer.gov/Data/Bioinformatics_Pipelines/DNA_Seq_Variant_Calling_Pipeline/#microsatellite-instability-detection).

Do not divide the retained coding count by the capture footprint and call the result clinical TMB. The present numerator retains possible germline variants and reflects converter/population-frequency filters; the denominator must be sample-callable coding territory under a specified coverage/quality rule. Variant classes, germline removal and assay calibration affect the value. The clinical 10 mutations/Mb convention is assay-linked and cannot be applied directly to this custom count. [Primary TMB harmonization study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7174078/); [FDA TMB approval summary](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8416776/).

## SWI/SNF and other secondary classifications

Retain TOV21G’s two ARID1A truncation candidates as genetic observations; two variants are not proof that they affect different alleles. Published experiments have characterized these same ARID1A frameshift positions, providing independent model context. Current total-protein detection does not establish an intact, functional protein. [Primary TOV21G ARID1A study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5159945/).

TOV112D’s SMARCA4 truncation and low protein, and low SMARCA4 protein in BIN67/COV434, support a SWI/SNF-oriented annotation. Published reassessment supports dedifferentiated carcinoma for TOV112D and SCCOHT for COV434. Keep that pathology evidence separate from abundance measurements; BIN67 does not have low SMARCA4 RNA despite low protein. VOA4841’s low SMARCA4 RNA/protein is an interesting follow-up lead, not sufficient evidence to change its histotype. [Primary histotype reassessment](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33328126/).

PIK3CA/PTEN/CTNNB1 candidates can be exposed in the same exact-variant table. ARID1A status alone should not be used as a surrogate for MSI or immunotherapy sensitivity. MSLN and TACSTD2 could be added to an exploratory target-abundance display at little computational cost, but there is no need to enlarge the main descriptor around them. Bulk CD274 in isolated tumour cultures cannot supply a tissue immune-cell-dependent score. Existing kallisto gene quantification is not a fusion assay; fusion-negative labels are therefore unsupported.

## Deliverable recommended for the Data Descriptor

Add one reusable, long-format annotation table and a short Usage Notes paragraph. Preserve continuous measurements and explicit assay availability across all 42 models. Suggested fields are model, patient, histotype, assay and passage, biomarker, exact allele/build/transcript, source and curated labels, read support, pathogenicity assertion/review date, origin status, biallelic status, relative copy measurements/bin support, RNA/protein units and ranks, protein feature/peptide/isoDoping metadata, and separate current-stock versus published-stock evidence. A clinical assay not performed should read **not assessed**, not negative.

Necessary work for that addition is limited to reproducible extraction, exact-allele curation, transparent evidence categories and provenance. MSI analysis is the most useful optional additional computational phenotype. Wet-lab receptor, MMR, HR and dependency assays are optional extensions unless the manuscript makes the corresponding validated classification. Do not require a broad treatment-response screen to complete a Data Descriptor.

Use patient-aware comparisons when comparing groups. Expression ranks include related models and histotype/centre composition; they are collection-relative, not population thresholds. Different passages and missing modalities prevent substituting another same-patient isolate as if it were the same measured specimen. Report unsupported copy-number negatives and unmeasured protein values as unknown.
