Author-selected draft for CHUM- and Ottawa-derived models, pending collaborator confirmation:

> Cells were maintained in OSE medium supplemented with 10% fetal bovine serum, 0.5 µg/mL amphotericin B, and 50 µg/mL gentamicin at 37 °C, 5% CO2, and 5% O2.

The nine models from patients 1369, 2295, and 3133 are described by [Létourneau et al. 2012](https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2407-12-379): OV1369-R2, TOV1369, OV2295, OV2295-R2, TOV2295-R, OV3133-R, OV3133-R2, TOV3133D, and TOV3133G. The paper reports 5% O2 and 5% CO2 with the supplemented OSE medium above. Solid-tumour cultures were established by scraping, and ascites cultures from the centrifuged cellular fraction.

[Fleury et al. 2015](https://doi.org/10.18632/genesandcancer.76) describes OV866-2, OV4453, OV4485, TOV3041G, and TOV3291G among its new models. It reports 7% O2 and 5% CO2 with supplemented OSE medium. [Sauriol et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.3390/cancers12082222) describes OV2085, TOV2414, TOV2835EP, TOV2881EP, TOV2929D, TOV3121EP, OV3291, OV3331, and TOV3392D among the models relevant here, with 37 °C, 7% O2, and 5% CO2. The latter paper describes OV3291 as not immortalised at the reported passages; confirm its status for the present samples. TOV3121D shares the patient number with TOV3121EP, but its specific derivation should be confirmed rather than inferred from the name.

[Ouellet et al. 2008](https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2407-8-152) is the derivation reference for OV1946. [Provencher et al. 2000](https://doi.org/10.1290/1071-2690%282000%29036%3C0357%3ACOFNEO%3E2.0.CO%3B2) describes OV90, TOV81D, TOV21G, and TOV112D. The later molecular reassignment of TOV112D and COV434 is documented by [Karnezis et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ygyno.2020.12.004). Historical histotype labels should not be presented as current pathological consensus.

The source of the OSE formulation is the CHUM literature; its extension to Ottawa is the author-selected draft, awaiting collaborator review. Published BIN67 alternatives differ: [Gamwell et al. 2013](https://doi.org/10.1186/1750-1172-8-33) used DMEM with 20% fetal calf serum and 20% Ham's F12, whereas [Orlando et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.59073), with Wang and Huntsman, used RPMI-1640 plus 10% fetal bovine serum at 37 °C and 5% CO2 for BIN67 and COV434. These papers cannot establish the protocol used for the current Ottawa stock. [Zhang et al. 2000](https://doi.org/10.1093/molehr/6.2.146) characterises COV434 under its historical granulosa-cell annotation.

The highlighted BC Cancer draft uses RPMI-1640 plus 10% fetal bovine serum at 37 °C and 5% CO2. The primary literature search found directly relevant evidence for COV434 in Orlando et al. and VOA4841 in [Zundell et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1158/0008-5472.CAN-21-1545). Zundell et al. also used these conditions for XVOA295, but that name is not sufficient to establish identity with the current VOA295 stock. It cites the primary-culture work by Bitler et al. involving Yemin Wang and David Huntsman ([2017](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncb3582)). The common formulation is a proposed starting point for the BC Cancer paragraph, not evidence that all 12 models were cultured identically.

Exact-name searches did not establish assay culture conditions for VOA10816, VOA12539, VOA14993, VOA6861, VOA4395, VOA8762, VOA8771, VOA5217, or VOA5436. A separate Wang/Huntsman study of VOA1066 used an initial 199:105 derivation medium but selected DMEM for subsequent experiments ([Wang et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0240412)). VOA1066 is an endometrial model outside this resource; those conditions were therefore not assigned to the present VOA models. Confirm the highlighted BC Cancer paragraph, oxygen and additional supplements, model-specific departures, derivation references, and the assay harvest records with the contributing laboratory.

For all models, confirm medium supplier and formulation, supplements, temperature, oxygen, CO2, passage at each harvest, identity of the harvested stock, and whether the RNA and protein aliquots came from the same culture. Existing passage metadata is useful but does not answer all of these questions.

## Proteomics protocol draft

**M03 — Confirm the highlighted acquisition and processing protocol with Negri/Morin.** Comment C7 requests that the existing protocol draft appear directly in Methods, highlighted and with its source. This is now implemented. The source is the 11-plex cell-pellet work in [Orlando et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.59073), involving Gian Luca Negri and Gregg Morin. The retained draft follows:

> Proteins from frozen cell pellets were digested with trypsin. Peptides were labelled with 11-plex tandem mass tags, pooled, separated by high-pH reversed-phase chromatography into 48 fractions, and concatenated into 12 fractions. Desalted peptides were analysed with an Easy-nLC 1000 coupled to an Orbitrap Fusion operating in MS3 mode. Spectra were searched with Sequest HT in Proteome Discoverer 2.1.1.21 against the UniProt reference proteome dated 3 August 2018. Precursor and fragment tolerances were 20 ppm and 0.8 Da. Oxidation and N-terminal acetylation were variable modifications; carbamidomethylation and TMT labelling were fixed modifications. Percolator filtering retained peptide-spectrum matches at a false-discovery rate below 1%.

Check the draft against the present acquisition records. Its cited study retained proteins supported by at least two unique peptides, whereas the current source table contains lower-support features; that published criterion was not added to the manuscript. Confirm lysis, reduction/alkylation, digestion amounts and timing, LC gradient, acquisition settings, protein-level false-discovery control, reporter interference thresholds, impurity correction, shared-peptide handling, protein summarisation, channel mapping, internal-standard composition, and all normalisation steps and scaling. Define the supplied CV column; bridge-derived repeatability is separately calculated. The tracked deletion of the standalone normalisation TODO in the abundance paragraph has been accepted. Normalisation remains part of the single full-protocol laboratory review, and the manuscript does not recast the supplied values as pure log2 ratios to the pooled standard.

Another relevant Morin/Negri paper is [Ji et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1002/path.6006), which profiles archival ovarian tumour tissue with SP3-CTP. It uses tissue-specific processing and is less directly applicable to cell pellets. Its accession PXD032355 belongs to that published study and must not be used as an accession for the present data.
